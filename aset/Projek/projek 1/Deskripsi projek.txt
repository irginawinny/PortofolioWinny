Sinta 3 Research Project

The research results show that the mathematics textbook integrating Problem-Based Learning (PBL), Ranggawarsita Museum ethnomathematics, and spreadsheet-assisted learning to enhance students’ statistical thinking skills. The research evaluated the textbook through feasibility, readability, student response, and effectiveness tests. The product achieved a 90.56% feasibility score, 86.58% readability score, and 95.91% positive student response.

The effectiveness test showed that students who used the textbook demonstrated significantly better statistical thinking skills than students receiving expository instruction (t = 5.31). More than 75% of students achieved mastery of the material (z = 1.969), while the textbook achieved an N-Gain score of 0.65, categorized as moderate (t = 4.178).
