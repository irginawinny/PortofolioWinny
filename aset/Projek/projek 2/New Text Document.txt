PKM-RSH Funded Project

Developed Mathchallenge, a mathematics textbook based on Challenge-Based Learning (CBL), enhanced with Virtual Reality (VR), and infused with cultural heritage buildings to improve students’ mathematical problem-solving skills. 

The Mathchallenge program achieved a 76% effectiveness rate during the observation period, demonstrating its potential to support students’ learning and problem-solving skills. The product also achieved an 86.97% feasibility score, categorized as highly feasible, an 89.58% readability score, categorized as easy to understand, and a 95% student response rate, categorized as very good.
